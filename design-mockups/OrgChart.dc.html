The daily flow, step by step

  1. An enquiry arrives in a shared mailbox.
  2. The two people on that mailbox are told. Whoever opens it first is shown as quoting.
  3. The desk forwards it to vendors from Outlook (GSAs, airlines, liners).
  4. Vendors reply into the same mailbox.
  5. The desk builds the quote from the rates received.
  6. The quote goes back to the agent or customer. The sent copy shows the enquiry is answered.
  7. Later the quote is marked won or lost in the Enquiry DB.

Who is told at each step

0 min: both people on that mailbox.
20 min, not opened: both again, louder.
60 min, not opened: the Manager, and the Owner's phone.
Problem on a shipment: the mailbox team and anyone copied.
