Your questions, answered

Are emails deleted? Never from the mailbox. Only the app's small card (sender, subject, a short preview) is removed: 7 days after the enquiry was opened or quoted, 30 days if nobody opened it. Search finds the real email at any time.

Contacts, saved or not

Each email shows a small "Contact saved" tick. Senders, and now the people you send to from Outlook (vendors, airlines), are added to the directory with their name and type. Admin confirms the type.

Circulars

A strip at the top of the Inbox says "Filed to Circulars today: 1" with the file name and what it was read as, so an airline circular never goes missing silently. If a circular arrives as plain text with no attachment, it is listed there as "Needs filing by hand".
