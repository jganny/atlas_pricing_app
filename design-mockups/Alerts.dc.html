Three levels, so it never gets messy

1. The bell. The complete list for that person, newest first, in two groups: Needs you and Updates. Nothing else in the app shows a count.
2. One strip above the Inbox list. Only what needs the user right now, in one line. When nothing is late, the strip is not there.
3. A pop-up on the phone or computer. Only for urgent things. Never for updates.

For you, on the road

The 60-minute alert now goes to ganny and "manager". If you are away, name the person who should act on it.
